# Privacy policy

Stagehand is published by Sundews. This policy covers the Stagehand app for Windows.

**Stagehand does not collect, send or share any information.** It has no account, no
analytics, no advertising and no crash reporting, and it never connects to the internet.
Everything it does happens on your own computer.

## What Stagehand uses while it runs

- **The picture of a window you share.** The Presenter Window copies what the window you
  chose shows into a second window on your own computer, so that you can share that one
  in a call. The picture is neither saved nor sent anywhere by Stagehand. What a calling
  app does with a window you share in it is covered by that app's own policy.
- **Mouse and pen movement.** While the laser pointer is on, Stagehand follows the
  pointer to draw the trail. The trail is only shown on screen and is not saved.
- **Key presses.** Stagehand listens for the shortcuts you have set. While you record a
  new shortcut in its settings, it also reads the keys you press in that window, to show
  and save the shortcut. No other typing is read, and nothing typed is saved apart from
  the shortcut itself.

## What Stagehand keeps on your computer

- **Your settings**, such as shortcuts, colours and which tools are on.
- **A log** to help find problems. It records when Stagehand and its tools start and
  stop, and errors. When you share a window, the log includes that window's title.

Both stay on your computer and are never sent anywhere. Installed from the Microsoft
Store, they are kept in Stagehand's own folder under `%LOCALAPPDATA%\Packages` and are
removed when you uninstall it. Run without installing, the settings are in
`%APPDATA%\Stagehand` and the log in `%LOCALAPPDATA%\Stagehand\logs`, which you can
delete at any time.

## The Microsoft Store

If you install Stagehand from the Microsoft Store, Microsoft handles the download and
updates, and may collect information as described in the
[Microsoft Privacy Statement](https://privacy.microsoft.com/privacystatement). Sundews
does not receive anything that identifies you.

## Changes and questions

Changes to this policy are published in this file, whose history shows what changed and
when. Stagehand is open source, so everything above can be checked in the code at
<https://github.com/hugener/Stagehand>. For questions, open an issue there.
